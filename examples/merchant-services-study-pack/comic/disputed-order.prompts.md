# Image prompts: The case of the disputed $100 order

One prompt per panel for an image model. Paste the character sheet into every prompt so the cast stays consistent.
Speech is listed separately: add the bubbles in a layout tool, since image models garble text.

## Character sheet

- **Maya** (runs a small online tea shop): woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron
- **Leo** (a shopper): man in his 20s, short brown hair, purple hoodie and a matching cap
- **Priya** (dispute analyst at the merchant's acquirer): woman in her 40s, long dark-brown hair, blue blazer, phone headset

Style: clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names

## Page 1: A normal sale

### Panel 1.1

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Leo on his couch buying a tin of tea on his phone. Setting: home. Characters: Leo: man in his 20s, short brown hair, purple hoodie and a matching cap. Props: phone. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: Day 0, 8 pm. Leo orders $100 of tea online.
- Leo (speech): "Pay now... done!"
- Teaches: Checkout: the shopper's card or wallet details go to the merchant's gateway.

### Panel 1.2

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Priya at her desk explaining how the authorization travels. Setting: office. Characters: Priya: woman in her 40s, long dark-brown hair, blue blazer, phone headset. Props: network, card. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: One second later.
- Priya (speech): "Our switch routes the auth by the card's BIN to the network, then to Leo's bank. It says yes and places a hold."
- Teaches: Authorization asks the issuer to stand behind the amount. No money moves yet.

### Panel 1.3

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Maya packing a box in her shop at the end of the day. Setting: shop. Characters: Maya: woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron. Props: store. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: Day 0, evening.
- Maya (speech): "Shipped! I'll capture it and the batch closes tonight."
- Teaches: Capture and batch close turn the auth into a deposit the acquirer settles through the networks.

### Panel 1.4

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Maya smiling at a laptop showing her bank deposit. Setting: shop. Characters: Maya: woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron. Props: money, bank. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: Day 1.
- Maya (speech): "The net deposit landed by ACH: sales minus fees."
- Teaches: Funding: the acquirer pays the merchant the net amount on the agreed schedule.

## Page 2: The dispute

### Panel 2.1

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Leo on the phone with his bank, worried. Setting: home. Characters: Leo: man in his 20s, short brown hair, purple hoodie and a matching cap. Props: phone. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: Five weeks later.
- Leo (speech): "I don't think my tea ever arrived. Can you dispute it?"
- Teaches: The cardholder disputes with the issuer, not with the merchant's acquirer.

### Panel 2.2

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Maya reading a chargeback notice, surprised. Setting: shop. Characters: Maya: woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron. Props: warning, doc. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: The issuer files a chargeback.
- Maya (shout): "$100 debited?!"
- Teaches: A first chargeback debits the merchant through the acquirer right away.

### Panel 2.3

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Priya on a video call with Maya, pointing at a tracking record. Setting: office. Characters: Maya: woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron; Priya: woman in her 40s, long dark-brown hair, blue blazer, phone headset. Props: doc, check. No text or lettering in the image.

- Priya (speech): "Send me the delivery tracking and the order record, before the deadline."
- Maya (speech): "Signed for on day 3. Here it is."
- Teaches: Representment: the merchant answers with evidence, sent through the acquirer by the network's deadline.

### Panel 2.4

> clean flat cartoon, thick dark outlines, soft pastel backgrounds, friendly faces, no logos or brand names. Scene: Maya relieved, Priya giving a thumbs up. Setting: shop. Characters: Maya: woman in her 30s, black hair in a bun, green shirt with a white apron; Priya: woman in her 40s, long dark-brown hair, blue blazer, phone headset. Props: scale, money. No text or lettering in the image.

- Caption: The issuer accepts the evidence.
- Priya (speech): "The $100 is credited back. If they push further, it's pre-arbitration, then the network decides."
- Teaches: If representment wins, the money comes back; otherwise the case can go to pre-arbitration and arbitration.

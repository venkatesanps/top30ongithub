# taste

Aesthetic judgment without a point of view is just preference. Make it explicit.

1. **Point of view:** one sentence on what this thing should feel like and for whom.
   "A calm, credible tool page for skeptical engineers" rules things out; "clean and modern"
   doesn't.
2. **Job of the form:** what the design, copy or name must do (be scanned in 5 seconds, build
   trust, be typed often, be remembered).
3. **Look at the concrete thing:** specific choices of type, spacing, colour, word, rhythm.
   Point at them; don't summarise.
4. **Effect:** what each choice does to the reader, good or bad, against the point of view.
5. **Strongest change:** the one revision with the biggest effect. Then the next two.

Avoid the defaults that make work look generated: gradient blobs, three identical cards,
"Unlock the power of", centred everything, emoji bullets. Name it when you see it.

Ledger: `point of view`, `job of the form`, `strongest change`, `carry`.
